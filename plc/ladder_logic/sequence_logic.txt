Automatic sequence representation

IDLE --(AUTO & RUN_LATCH)--> STARTUP
STARTUP --(MOTOR_FEEDBACK)--> CONVEYING
CONVEYING --(ENTRY_RISING)--> PRODUCT_DETECTED
PRODUCT_DETECTED --(POSITION_SENSOR)--> POSITIONING
POSITIONING --(SORT_LATCH)--> SORTING
POSITIONING --(EXIT_RISING)--> PRODUCT_EXIT
SORTING --(EXIT_RISING)--> PRODUCT_EXIT
PRODUCT_EXIT --(EXIT_CLEAR)--> CONVEYING

Any state --(E_STOP)--> EMERGENCY_STOP
Any state --(LATCHED_FAULT)--> FAULT
